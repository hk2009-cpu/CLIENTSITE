About

Duck's Nest Preschool is a nurturing and inclusive early childhood education center dedicated to fostering the growth and development of young children. Our experienced staff provides a safe and stimulating environment where children can explore, learn, and build essential social and cognitive skills. We believe in the importance of play-based learning and encourage creativity, curiosity, and a love for learning in every child.

Each campus offers a unique learning experience, with dedicated teachers and age-appropriate activities designed to meet the needs of each child.
