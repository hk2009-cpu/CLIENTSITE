FAQs

  1. What are the operating hours of Duck's Nest Preschool?

    2. Duck's Nest Preschool is open from 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday through Friday.

  2. What is the student-to-teacher ratio at Duck's Nest Preschool?

    3. The student-to-teacher ratio at Duck's Nest Preschool is 1:4-5 for our youngest learners and 1:6-7 for older children.

  3. What is the curriculum like at Duck's Nest Preschool?

    4. Our curriculum is play-based and designed to foster creativity, curiosity, and a love for learning in every child.

  4. How do I enroll my child at Duck's Nest Preschool?

    5. To enroll your child, please contact us directly to schedule a visit and complete the enrollment forms.

  5. How does Duck's Nest handle challenging behavior?

    6. We approach challenging behavior with patience and understanding, focusing on positive reinforcement and clear communication with both children and parents.
